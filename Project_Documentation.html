1. Executive Summary & Project Purpose

GestureSync AI (repository: SignLangTran) is a real-time, multimodal artificial intelligence communication suite engineered to dissolve the communicative barrier between Deaf/Hard-of-Hearing individuals and the hearing society.

Core Problem Statement

Over 70 million individuals worldwide communicate primarily through Sign Language (ASL, ISL, BSL). In everyday life—such as emergency triage, banking, law enforcement, and educational spaces—non-signers are incapable of understanding sign gestures. Meanwhile, professional interpreters are scarce, costly, and unavailable during spontaneous daily interactions.

4. Computer Vision & Kinematic Classification

GestureSync AI employs a 21-point hand skeletal landmark model. Each hand consists of 21 3D spatial coordinates normalized relative to the camera frame:

LANDMARK KEYPOINTS TOPOLOGY: 0: Wrist Anchor 1-4: Thumb (CMC, MCP, IP, Tip) 5-8: Index Finger (MCP, PIP, DIP, Tip) 9-12: Middle Finger (MCP, PIP, DIP, Tip) 13-16: Ring Finger (MCP, PIP, DIP, Tip) 17-20: Pinky Finger (MCP, PIP, DIP, Tip) CLASSIFIER FORMULA: Finger_Extended = Dist(Wrist, Tip) > Dist(Wrist, PIP) * 1.15 Thumb_Angle = Cos^-1( (v_thumb . v_index) / (|v_thumb| * |v_index|) )

5. Backend & Deep Learning Architecture Roadmap

1. Edge Client Inference (Current): Runs 100% in browser via MediaPipe WebAssembly. Zero server bandwidth costs, zero video transmission, sub-15ms latency, complete data privacy.

2. Production Enterprise Backend (Roadmap): FastAPI (Python 3.11) with WebSockets transmitting landmark coordinate vectors. Temporal Spatial-Temporal Graph Convolutional Networks (ST-GCN) or LSTM models for multi-frame sentence classification, paired with lightweight T5 NLP grammar smoothing and PostgreSQL data persistence.

6. Complete End-to-End Operational Workflow

1

Video Capture & Neural Preprocessing

Webcam captures 60 FPS video stream. MediaPipe Hands locates hand regions and extracts 21 3D spatial coordinate vectors.

2

Euclidean Kinematic Pose Classification

Kinematic engine evaluates finger extensions, thumb opposition angles, and relative knuckle distances to classify signs in <15ms.

3

Grammar Smoothing & Lexical Buffer

Raw ASL glosses (e.g. HELLO THANK YOU) are buffered and mapped into natural, fluent prose sentences.

4

Multimodal Delivery & Voice Vocalization

Direct output card below webcam updates text immediately. Browser Web Speech API synthesizes natural audio output through device speakers.

5

Reverse Channel: Speech-to-Sign Playback

Hearing participants speak into microphone. Speech recognition converts audio into tokens, which are rendered as sequential animated sign cards.
